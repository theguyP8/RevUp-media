Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in each website package?

Every package includes a custom-designed website, responsive mobile optimization, foundational SEO structure, high-speed hosting, and ongoing monthly maintenance. Page counts, dynamic blogs, and advanced funnel architectures scale with your chosen tier.

How long does it take until my site goes live?

Standard builds go live in 10 to 20 business days once you complete our onboarding form and submit your branding and content assets.

Why is there an ongoing monthly fee?

The monthly fee covers ultra-fast cloud server hosting, SSL encryption, daily backups, 24/7 uptime monitoring, security patching, and regular content adjustments without unpredictable hourly invoices.

What changes are covered under ongoing maintenance?

Routine updates to copy, images, business hours, and promotions are fully covered under your monthly subscription. Structural platform rebuilds or adding completely new feature engines are quoted separately.
